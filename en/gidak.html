Guides

Quick guides: choosing a machine, preparing 3D files and studying in a private room.

How to choose a machine

Office, gaming or high performance? For writing, studying or programming, the i5 with 16 GB is plenty. For gaming, pick Entry, Pro or Elite depending on the resolution and refresh rate you want. For rendering or video editing, the 64 GB high-performance machine is the right choice.

Learn more

Prepare your file for 3D printing

Export your model as STL or 3MF, check that walls are at least 1.2 mm thick and that the model has no gaps. Tell us the size, colour and intended use: we’ll quote the price (PLA, €0.35/g) before we start.

Learn more

Studying in a private room

Book in 2–3 hour blocks, bring headphones and keep your phone off the desk. The private room (+€1.50/hour) cuts the noise and is ideal for exams, video calls or small-group work.
