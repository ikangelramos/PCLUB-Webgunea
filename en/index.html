Any questions?

The questions our users ask most often.

What is PCLUB?

It’s a club in Errenteria where you can use computers by the hour. You pick the type of machine to suit your activity: office, gaming or high performance.

Which operating systems are available?

The office area offers Windows 11 Home and Ubuntu. Gaming and high-performance machines run Windows 11 Pro.

Do you have gaming computers?

Yes. We have three gaming tiers (Entry, Pro and Elite), from RTX 4060 to 4080 and 144 Hz to 360 Hz. They also work well for 3D design or video editing.

Can I book a private room?

Yes, we have private rooms for solo use or small groups: for work, study, online meetings or gaming with friends. Book by phone or just drop in.

How much does it cost?

Office €2.50/hour · Gaming €3.50–5.50/hour · High performance €6.00/hour. Packs and special night rates are also available. See the Computers page for details.
