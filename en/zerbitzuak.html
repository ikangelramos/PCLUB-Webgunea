3D printing

A service for creating three-dimensional parts without buying your own 3D printer. PLA material.

What can you use 3D printing for?

If you have the design, we’ll print it. If not, we’ll help you prepare one.

  • Design projects
  • Making prototypes
  • Study projects
  • Custom parts

How to order 3D parts

  1. Bring your design

    In STL or 3MF format. If you don’t have one, we’ll help you prepare it.

  2. Agree on the details

    We’ll agree on size, material (PLA) and colour.

  3. We print

    We make the part on our 3D printer.

  4. Collect it

    When your part is ready, come and pick it up.
